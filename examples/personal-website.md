# Danang Siseka — Personal Website

> A personal website and digital portfolio serving as a central hub for work, writing, research, achievements, and digital presence.

## ✦ Overview

This website is the personal website and digital portfolio of **Danang Siseka**.

It is designed as a long-term personal archive and digital identity hub, bringing together work and information that may otherwise be distributed across different platforms.

The website documents not only completed work, but also ongoing learning, exploration, and development.
